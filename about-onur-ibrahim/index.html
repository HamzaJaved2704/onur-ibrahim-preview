About Onur

I know the weight of running a business.

I’ve built and sold businesses of my own. I know what it’s like when the money, the team and the next big decision all rest on you.

Today, I help business owners get better results from sales and marketing. I work beside them to choose what needs to change and help their people get it done.

From large agencies to my own businesses.

My career includes agency work for brands such as Land Rover, Ford, Nokia and Tesco.

That taught me how much depends on a clear brief, good data and people who know what they need to do.

Building my own businesses taught me the other side. A plan has to work with the time, cash and people you have.

I bring both views to the work I do now.

I help you choose the next move and see it through.

Sometimes the offer needs work. Sometimes sales are lost through slow replies or a poor handover.

And sometimes good people are busy doing work that will not solve the real problem.

I help you see what is happening, choose where to focus and agree who does what next.

That can mean advice, oversight of your agencies and team, or delivery by my own team.

The way I work.

I ask clear questions, look at the numbers and tell you what I think.

When something works, we build on it. When it fails, we look at why. If a problem needs a skill outside mine, I bring in the right person.

I’ve trained in martial arts since I was seven, and I still teach. That habit carries into business: get the basics right, practise, and keep learning from what happens.

Let’s start with your business.

Tell me what you want to change and what you have tried so far.

We’ll use the first hour to talk it through and see where I might help.
