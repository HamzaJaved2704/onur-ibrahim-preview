THE PROBLEM

Get more from the resources and people you already pay for.

It’s hard to know what needs to change when your agency says one thing and your sales team says another.

I look at what’s being done, what it costs and what it leads to. Then I help your people focus on the changes that matter.

That could mean a clearer offer, faster replies to new enquiries, or a better plan for your agency. You get clear next steps, someone in charge of each task and a way to check progress.

I help find the holes.

Which enquiries turn into paying clients?

If you can see clicks and leads but not which ones become clients, the budget is hard to judge. I look at the path from first click to sale, so we can see which work deserves more of your time and money.

Where do good enquiries go quiet?

An enquiry may wait too long for a reply, a quote or a clear next step. I check the handover and follow up with your team. Then we agree who owns the next action and how to see progress.

What happens when work passes to the next person?

Ask where customer information, promises or instructions get lost between people. I help you review those handovers and agree what good looks like. If the issue needs a delivery specialist, we bring one in.

Does each sale leave enough profit?

Sales can grow while profit stays flat. We can look at pricing, discounts and the cost to serve. Where we need a deeper financial review, I work with your accountant or the right specialist.

Which decisions still wait for you?

If small choices keep coming back to you, progress slows. We look at roles, ownership and the decisions your team can make. Where the issue needs HR or specialist support, I bring in the right person.
