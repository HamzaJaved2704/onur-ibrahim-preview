ACQUISITIONS

Thinking of selling your business?

I’m looking to buy established UK businesses in B2B services, health, wellness and leisure, with annual revenue of £2 million or more.

You may be ready to sell, or just starting to think about stepping back. Let’s talk about the business you have built and what you want to happen next.

Send a private enquiry

A first conversation helps us see whether there could be a fit.

Start with what matters to you.

The price matters. So do your team, your clients and the life you want after a sale.

I want to understand those things before we talk about a deal.

You may want a full exit or time to hand things over. We can discuss both.

The businesses I’m looking for.

I’m interested in established UK firms with a skilled team and clients who return or need the service on an ongoing basis.

The main sectors are B2B services, health, wellness and leisure, with annual revenue of £2 million or more.

The business may still rely on you. Systems may need work. I want to understand what works well and what needs support.

I know what it takes to let go.

I’ve built and sold businesses of my own.

As a buyer, my aim is to build on what works and give the business a sound next stage.

We would talk through your team, key clients, the business name and any role you might keep.

You should be able to question the plan and judge whether I am the right next owner.

Clear about the deal and the funding.

Any proposal needs to show what is paid on completion, what may come later and what those payments depend on.

I’ll explain the proposed funding route and any finance that still needs approval.

You and your advisers can weigh up the whole deal before agreeing terms.

A clear path from first talk to handover.

01

First, we talk.

You tell me about the business, why you are thinking of a change and what matters most.

02

Then, we agree what to share.

If we both want to go further, we agree confidentiality terms before you share sensitive details.

03

We explore the deal.

We discuss the business, funding, proposed terms and handover. We make clear what still needs checking.

04

We check and complete.

The right advisers review the business, finances and legal position. If both sides proceed and conditions are met, we complete the sale.

Any role you keep after the sale has agreed work, time and pay.

You can involve your advisers.

Your broker, accountant or solicitor is welcome in the conversation.

You can take time to ask questions and get advice from people who act for you.

Let’s start with a short note.

Tell me what the business does and what you are considering. You can leave accounts and sensitive details for later.

I usually reply within two working days.
