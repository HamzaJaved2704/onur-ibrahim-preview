Three ways I can help.

01

Set the plan.

You may be spending more but winning less work. Or sales have slowed and you are unsure where to start.

I look at your offer, how you win work and what happens after an enquiry arrives.

Together, we choose what to fix first and set a clear plan. Each step has someone in charge, a due date and a way to check the result.

You can bring me in for a focused review or ongoing advice. We agree the scope and time before we start.

Fee: Priced after the first hour, once we’ve agreed the scope and time.

02

Guide your agencies and team.

You already have people doing the work. You want to know they have the right plan and are giving you value.

I check the brief, review the work and ask what the numbers show. We agree who owns each next step and when it is due.

Good people may need clearer goals, a better handover or help to see what is going wrong.

My role is to help them focus and keep you clear on progress.

Fee: Priced after the first hour, based on the time and review schedule we agree.

03

Let my team do the work.

When you need people to put the plan into action, my team can help.

That could mean ads, a website, email follow up or better tracking.

We agree what needs doing, who will do it and how we will judge the result. I stay involved to keep the work tied to your goals.

Fee: Priced after the first hour, once we’ve agreed the work and what it includes.

Know what you are paying for.

My advice and oversight have their own scope and fee. Delivery, ad spend, tools and outside help are set out in the proposal where they apply.

You can see the full cost before you decide.

For some businesses, we can discuss a lower cash fee alongside equity or an agreed share of growth.
