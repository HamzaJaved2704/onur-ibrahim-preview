Growth partnerships

Grow your business with someone who shares in the result.

You have built a sound business. You can see room to grow, but too much still rests on you.

I work beside owners to set the plan, guide the people doing the work and track the result.

We can agree equity, a share of revenue growth, or a lower cash fee alongside a share.

Ask about a growth partnership

Our first conversation is free. We use it to see whether there could be a fit.

Choose the model that fits the work.

Equity.

For owners who want a lasting partner and are open to sharing ownership.

The usual range is 5% to 15%, earned over two to three years as I meet agreed milestones.

We set out my role, time, rights and how shares are earned. We also agree what happens if we stop working together.

Revenue share.

For owners who want to keep their shares and link my return to revenue growth.

The current model is 10% of revenue above an agreed starting figure for 24 months, with a cap agreed up front.

We measure it from your accounts and review it each quarter.

For example, £20,000 above that figure in a quarter would mean a £2,000 share, subject to the agreed terms.

A lower cash fee can sit alongside either model. A profit share would need its own agreed terms.

Know the full cost before you decide.

More sales should leave the business stronger. So we look at margins, the cost of growth and my share together.

We also set out what your team will need to do, plus any spend on tools or outside help.

You can compare the full proposal with a fixed fee before choosing.

The businesses this suits.

  • I look for established businesses in B2B services, health, wellness and leisure, with annual revenue from £500,000 to £5 million.
  • There needs to be profit, or a clear path to it, and a team able to carry out the work.
  • This suits an owner who plans to stay and lead for at least three more years.
  • You will need to share the numbers, make decisions and work with me on the changes we agree.
